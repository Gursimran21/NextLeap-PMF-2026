### Mapping Business Outcomes to Product Outcomes: Swiggy's Challenge with Customer Support Queries

1. Swiggy is India's leading food ordering and delivery platform. Since its launch in 2014, Swiggy has grown rapidly and currently has a presence in over 500 cities across India. Swiggy's mission is to provide a seamless food ordering and delivery experience to its customers.

2. Swiggy's app has been widely popular among users due to its ease of use and efficient delivery system. However, Swiggy has been facing a challenge with the increasing number of customer support queries. This has led to a backlog of queries and increased wait times for customers seeking assistance.
   
3. Swiggy's management team has identified reducing the number of customer support queries as a key priority. They believe that reducing the number of queries will not only improve customer satisfaction but also increase operational efficiency and reduce costs.
   
4. Swiggy's management team has decided to address this challenge by mapping the business outcomes to relevant product outcomes. They believe that by identifying the root causes of the high volume of customer support queries, they can develop targeted solutions to address them.
   
5. Swiggy's management team is now seeking the input of the product team to identify the most effective approach to reducing the number of customer support queries. The product team will need to analyse the data, as well as conduct user research to understand the root causes of the high volume of customer support queries.

6. The product team will also need to develop a roadmap for implementing the chosen solutions. This roadmap should include specific product outcomes that are aligned with the business outcomes of reducing the number of customer support queries.
   
### Question:

You are the PM on the customer support team.

1. Can you estimate the number of customer support agents that Swiggy needs to have?
2. What would be the different product outcomes that your roadmap will have?
